# PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a production-ready SaaS platform called **AdWallet Africa**.

The platform allows African businesses, creators, churches, NGOs, agencies, and entrepreneurs to fund advertising campaigns using Mobile Money without needing an international credit card.

Users can deposit funds through Mobile Money and receive Ad Credits (tokens) that can be used to launch campaigns on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Google, and YouTube.

The system should look modern, premium, and fintech-grade, similar to Stripe, Brex, Linear, Notion, and Ramp.
